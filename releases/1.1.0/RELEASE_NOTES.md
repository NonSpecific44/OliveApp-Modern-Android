### New
- **Home-screen widget.** Shows your Olive's current track with its artwork, plus previous, play/pause and next. Long-press your home screen, choose **Widgets → Olive**, and place it. It's 4×1 by default; resize it taller for a larger layout with bigger artwork.
- **Album art in the now-playing bar** at the bottom of the app, in place of the generic icon.

### Improved
- Every change is now built and tested automatically on GitHub, which also checks that each published APK was built from the source in the repository.

### Install
Update from inside the app (**Settings → Check for updates**), or download `OliveApp-modern-1.1.0.apk` and open it. It installs over 1.0.0 and earlier modern builds, keeping your settings.
